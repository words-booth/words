The Age of

False Treasure

Anxiety without identifiable cause,
the fruits of a world lacking pause.
Rushing towards life without a point.

Service surrendered to devices,
attention distracted—
an everlasting flood of vices.

Peace helplessly sought in consumption,
puppeteered through false ideals,
constantly chasing the feels,
suffering swallowed as norm—
tired bird catching only illusory worm.

Wise owl detached from distraction,
remaining focused on the inner attraction.

Filled with love beyond measure,
shedding tears for a world
ensnared in false treasure.
