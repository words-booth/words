The Abyss

Born into an ocean of suffering.
Tossed by the tidewaters of nescience.

Conformity — the only lifeboat in sight.
A mighty rock tumbled into a mediocre pebble.

Some cling to the surface,
blindsided by the cliff of annihilation.
Some venture deeper, unafraid of the abyss.

Those wise souls find comfort in the void.
Pleasure in its silence.
Indifferent to darkness,
they discover bliss from within.

May their love illuminate the depths in us all.
